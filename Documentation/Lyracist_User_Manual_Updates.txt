   - Player cards dynamically tint their background and borders according to strike accumulation:

4. ⚡ Super Streak Rotary Target Wheel:
   - Achieving a consecutive streak of 20 correct answers unlocks the Super Streak challenge.
   - Pauses normal gameplay and opens a Scaryoke-style rotary wheel populated with slices for all active opponents currently holding shield tokens.

Intelligent Name & Artist Capitalization Engine (`All Applications`) - (August 2026)

Section: Performance & Rotation Operations / Performer & Song Data Entry (`Lyracist`, `KSRotation`, `KSRotation.Maui`, `LyracistDbEditor`, `ScaryokeWheel`)

[Smart Capitalization Rules]
1. Mixed-Case & Stylized Performer Names (e.g. "DeaR", "McDonald", "LeBron", "MacDonald"):
   - The system automatically capitalizes the initial letter of every word while intelligently preserving intentional internal and trailing capital letters typed by users (such as "DeaR", "LeBron", or "vanBuren") rather than forcing them to lowercase.
2. Apostrophe Name Prefixes (e.g. "O'Neal", "D'Angelo", "L'Amour", "M'Baku"):
   - When entering Irish, Scottish, Italian, or French names with single-letter prefixes and apostrophes (e.g. "o'neal", "O'neal", "O'NEAL", "d'angelo"), the system automatically capitalizes both the prefix and the root surname ("O'Neal", "D'Angelo", "L'Amour").
3. Scottish & Irish "Mc" Prefixes (e.g. "McDonald", "McCartney"):
   - Surnames starting with "Mc" are automatically recognized and capitalized with the secondary root capital ("McDonald", "McCartney").
4. Hyphenated Names & Titles:
   - Compound names separated by hyphens (e.g. "mary-ann smith", "smith-o'neal") are capitalized on all segment boundaries ("Mary-Ann Smith", "Smith-O'Neal").
5. Acronyms, Roman Numerals & Common Contractions:
   - Preserves standard music acronyms and Roman numerals (e.g. "DJ", "MC", "II", "III", "IV", "VIII") and cleanly formats contractions in song titles (e.g. "Don't Stop Believin'", "Rock 'N' Roll").

Auto-Advance System for Karaoke Hosting (`Lyracist`) - (August 2026)

[System Details]
1. Safe, DJ-Friendly Auto-Advance Lifecycle:
   - **Post-Performance Grace Period**:
     * When a karaoke track finishes naturally, the playback engine triggers a configurable grace period countdown (default 15 seconds, adjustable from 5s to 30s in Settings).
     * During the countdown, the fill-in background music player spins up automatically at a pleasant ducked volume level.
     * The stage billboard displays the upcoming performer notice: `"Next singer: {name} — please come to the stage"`.
   - **Large High-Contrast DJ Control Buttons**:
     * **`▶ START SONG`** (`StartSongButton`): Allows the DJ to immediately start the next performer without waiting for the grace timer to run down. It stops the grace timer, halts/fades out fill-in music, loads the singer's song, synchronizes the lyrics projection window, mobile tablet lyrics server, and rotation billboard, and begins track playback.
     * **`⏭ SKIP SINGER`** (`SkipSingerButton`): Skips the current performer if they stepped away or are not ready, advancing the rotation without counting the song as completed or saving to history, while keeping fill-in music playing and restarting the grace period countdown for the next performer in line.
   - **State Machine Safeguards (`AutoAdvanceState`)**:
     * `StartingSong` transition lock prevents accidental double-clicks from double-starting tracks.
     * Auto-advance automatically inhibits itself during active Trivia and Scaryoke games to prevent song interruption.
     * If the queue is empty, the system returns safely to `Idle`.
     * If the next performer has not selected a song, the system transitions to `WaitingForSongSelection` with a stage prompt and keeps fill-in music playing.

[Update Details]
A dynamic tiered scoring system has been integrated into Lyracist, KSRotation, and Lyracist.Trivia, tying point awards directly to the visual option elimination phases during live question play.

1. Progressive Option Elimination & Value Tiers:
   - **4 Options Visible (0 Eliminated)**: 100% of Base Points (1,000 pts default). Rewarding early, confident buzz-ins before any wrong answers fade.

   - Real-time Point Multiplier Pill (`#value-multiplier-pill`) prominently displayed above the buzzer grid.
